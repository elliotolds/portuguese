These are ANKI decks and other materials to help me prepare to pass the EU Portuguese CIPLE A2 exam. I only care about EU Portuguese, so don't mention Brazilian Portuguese in any of your notes in the ANKI decks or to me.

googlesheetPT.csv is not a normal spreasheet. It's sort of a visual review sheet where I have multiple "sheets within a sheet". Most sub-sheets are only three columns long (the PT word, the english word, then any notes), and then another sub-sheet will start a column or two after that for a separate type of word. So you can't expect all columns within a row to relate to each other. Never insert rows in the middle of the file — sub-sheets are column-aligned, not row-aligned, so inserting shifts unrelated sub-sheets out of sync.

cheatsheet_general.md is the replacement for googlesheetPT.csv, which is now deprecated.

Elliot_PT_UberA2.txt is my main ANKI deck and my main source of truth.

Elliot_PT_irregular_verbs2.txt and Elliot_PT_regular_verbs.txt are also used to practice verb conjugations.

Irregular verbs in Elliot_PT_irregular_verbs2.txt Elliot_PT_UberA2.txt have conjugation tables in the notes sections. The rules for these conjugation tables is the same, so for the same word in both decks the tables should be identical. The rules are:
-If an entry in the table is the same as what it'd be if the verb were regular, don't do any bolding or coloring.
-If at least 3 out of the 5 rows for a given tense would be regular if we assume they that the verb tense had a different stem from what it actually has, then make these entries bold. For example the simple past version of trazer has the stem troux, but only the tu, nós, and elas/eles forms are just bold here because they're the only ones that are regular assuming the stem was troux. The Eu and ele/ela/você forms are different from how the simple past of a regular verb with the stem troux would be conjugated.
-If any entry is not predictable either by being regular or by being regular if you assume a different 'stem' which matches at least 3/5 entries in this tense, then make it both bold and blue. In the previous example, both the Eu and Ele/Ela/Você simple past forms of trazer would be blue and bold.
