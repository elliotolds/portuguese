

These are ANKI decks and other materials to help me prepare to pass the EU Portuguese CIPLE A2 exam. I only care about EU Portuguese, so don't mention Brazilian Portuguese in any of your notes in the ANKI decks or to me.

googlesheetPT.csv is not a normal spreasheet. It's sort of a visual review sheet where I have multiple "sheets within a sheet". Most sub-sheets are only three columns long (the PT word, the english word, then any notes), and then another sub-sheet will start a column or two after that for a separate type of word. So you can't expect all columns within a row to relate to each other. Never insert rows in the middle of the file — sub-sheets are column-aligned, not row-aligned, so inserting shifts unrelated sub-sheets out of sync.

Elliot_PT_UberA2.txt is my main ANKI deck and my main source of truth. 

Elliot_PT_irregular_verbs2.txt and Elliot_PT_regular_verbs.txt are also used to practice verb conjugations.
